JAN 2026

IN PURSUIT OF SKILL

The other day I was watching a video of Elon Musk eating pineapple pizza with Joe Rogan and one moment caught my interest. Elon was talking about how his son asked him what Los Angeles was like ± 4000 years.

I pondered on this question for a while. Obviously the future will be wild beyond our imagination. But will that future actually look like? My intuition said that in 4000 years if we’re still around, we will have built the tools that give us the abilities of the gods we worshiped from the prior 4000 years. I imagine a world with teleportation-like transportation, von-neumann probes and immortality.
